Second Look

A visual second opinion on your PowerPoint decks — built for blind and low-vision professionals.

Upload a PowerPoint file and Second Look will:

  • Measure what is on each slide — positions, sizes, fonts, alt text, and reading order.
  • Describe how each slide looks using AI, with a confidence level for every opinion.
  • List problems ranked by severity.
  • Answer questions about your deck.
